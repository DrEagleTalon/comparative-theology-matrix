👤 About Me

My Mission

I'm passionate about creating interactive tools that make complex comparative research accessible and visual. This site hosts a collection of theological matrices, philosophical frameworks, and research utilities designed to help you understand different worldviews and belief systems side by side.

What You'll Find Here

Comparative matrices for theology and belief systems, interactive learning tools, philosophical frameworks, and original research. All tools are open-source and designed for educators, students, researchers, and anyone curious about comparative religion and philosophy.
